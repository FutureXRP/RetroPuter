Advertise on RetroPuter!

RetroPuter is a computer museum you can actually use: turn on a real-feeling computer from 1985, 1990, 1995 or 2000, dial in with a screeching modem, surf the early Web and play the games people played. Families, kids and grown-ups who remember dial-up all visit.

*** ALL ADS ON RETROPUTER LOOK AND FEEL RETRO *** NO MODERN BANNERS *** THINK 1985 TO 2000 ***

Where your ad goes

Ads appear on the desk view: the room every visitor sees first, with the computer sitting on the desk. It shows when they arrive, while the computer starts up, and when they shut down or travel to another year. Ads never cover the computer's screen, so they never get in the way of playing.

The one rule: make it retro

Your ad should look like it belongs on a computer from 1985 to 2000, like a banner from the early Web or a magazine ad from the era.

  • Do: pixel art, chunky system fonts, bright 16- and 256-color palettes, bevelled buttons, "CLICK HERE!", starry backgrounds, simple GIF-style animation.
  • Do: have fun with the era. A 1985 green-screen ad, a 1995 CD-ROM ad or a Y2K ad are all welcome.
  • Don't: modern photography, glossy gradients, video, sound that plays on its own, or anything that covers the page.
  • Don't: flash rapidly (keep blinking gentle), or make ads that pretend to be part of the computer or a system message.
  • Don't: anything not suitable for kids. RetroPuter is family-friendly.
