Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

The short version (for kids) RetroPuter doesn't have accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or where you live. Your games, money, drawings and notes are saved only on the computer or phone you're using. The chat buddies are computer characters, not real people. Never type your real name, address or phone number anywhere online (RetroPuter hides numbers that look like phone numbers anyway).

This policy explains what information RetroPuter ("we", "the site") handles when you visit RetroPuter, a website that lets you use pretend computers from 1985 to 2000. We wrote it in plain language. If anything is unclear, contact us (see the end of this page).

1. We don't collect personal information

  • There are no accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, age, address, phone number or photo.
  • We don't run analytics, tracking pixels or fingerprinting, and we don't sell or share information about visitors. If that ever changes, we'll update this page first.
  • The "user name" inside the pretend computer (like kidsurfer) is just for fun and stays on your device.

2. What's saved, and where

Everything you do on RetroPuter is saved in your own web browser's storage (called "localStorage") on your device. It is not sent to us, and we can't see it. This includes:

  • play money, games you've "bought", coupon unlocks and settings
  • notes, letters, spreadsheets, drawings, photos taken in Photo Studio, movies, banners and home pages
  • high scores, Time Passport stamps, Work Center progress
  • what you've told the pretend chat buddies (so they "remember" you). Text that looks like a phone number, email or address is hidden and not saved.

To delete it: use Control Panel → Erase hard drive inside RetroPuter, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Private or incognito windows delete it automatically when closed.

Backups and sharing are up to you. A save file or backup code you create contains your saved progress, and it only goes where you send it. Links you share for movies or home pages contain that movie or page inside the link itself, so anyone you give the link to can see it.

4. Microphone and speech

  • Sound Recorder (1995 and 2000) asks your browser for permission before using the microphone. Recordings stay in memory while the program is open, are never uploaded or saved, and disappear when you close it.
  • "Read aloud" features use your device's built-in speech voice. The text is not sent to us.
  • Photo Studio never uses your real camera. Its pictures are of pretend places.

5. Children's privacy

RetroPuter is made for everyone, including children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and there's no way to create an account or send us information through the site. The pretend chat, mail and games room contain only computer characters, and there is no way to talk to real people through RetroPuter.

Parents: you can review everything saved by opening the pretend computer, and erase it any time (see section 2). If you believe your child has sent us personal information another way, contact us and we'll delete it.

6. Cookies and your choices

RetroPuter itself doesn't use cookies. It uses your browser's storage only to save your progress, which the site needs in order to work.
